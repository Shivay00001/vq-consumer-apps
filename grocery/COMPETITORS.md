# Competitor Research — VQ Grocery

Research date: 2026-09-26. Prices are per cited sources; verify before quoting to partners.

## Competitors

### 1. Blinkit (Zomato/Eternal)
- **Model:** 10-minute quick commerce via dark stores.
- **Seller pricing:** ₹25,000 per SKU per state onboarding fee (reported by Economic Times, citing seller accounts); ~10–20% commission per category (Unicommerce seller guide 2026); GST registration mandatory for sellers.
- **Consumer pricing:** free app; delivery + handling + small platform fee per order; surge in peak hours.
- **Copy:** sub-10-minute ops discipline, dark-store inventory accuracy, SKU-level search.
- **Beat:** onboarding cost is the #1 seller complaint — VQ Grocery has zero onboarding fee and a 5–8% commission target.

### 2. Zepto
- **Model:** 10-minute quick commerce, dark-store network.
- **Seller pricing:** reported ad commitments of ₹2–7.5 lakh/month for new/small brands (Economic Times); nominal onboarding fee ₹2–5 lakh in some reports for dark-store "Growth Partner" program (varies by city — confirm with Zepto's partner team).
- **Copy:** speed-first UX, category landing pages, replacement/refund flow.
- **Beat:** same as Blinkit — seller economics; plus VQ Grocery serves kirana stores Zepto will never onboard.

### 3. Swiggy Instamart
- **Model:** quick commerce inside the Swiggy app.
- **Seller pricing:** reported three-tier onboarding packages ₹4.5–9 lakh (adjustable against ad spend over 3 months) (Economic Times).
- **Copy:** app-within-app distribution, shared delivery fleet economics.
- **Beat:** onboarding packages are prohibitive for single-store grocers; VQ's pitch is "your store, online, today, free".

### 4. BigBasket (Tata)
- **Model:** scheduled + express grocery delivery; BB Now for quick delivery.
- **Seller/brand pricing:** not publicly disclosed; category commission negotiated per brand (sources report 15–25% typical for marketplace grocery models).
- **Copy:** scheduled slots (planning-friendly), large assortment, Tata trust / BB Star membership.
- **Beat:** BigBasket's assortment is a strength we don't chase — we chase "my local store" loyalty + lower fees.

### 5. JioMart
- **Model:** value grocery, kirana-partnered fulfilment (JioMart Partner kirana network).
- **Pricing:** free consumer app, low delivery fees, aggressive price-led positioning.
- **Copy:** kirana-as-fulfilment-node is the closest strategic precedent to VQ Grocery's model.
- **Beat:** JioMart's kirana partners complain of thin margins and platform control; VQ offers the store its own branded storefront and customer data.

## Feature matrix

| Feature | Blinkit | Zepto | Instamart | BigBasket | JioMart | **VQ Grocery (target)** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Delivery promise | ~10 min | ~10 min | ~10 min | Slots / express | 1–2 days | Same-day slots |
| Seller onboarding fee | ₹25k/SKU/state | High ad commit | ₹4.5–9L pkgs | Negotiated | Low | **₹0** |
| Commission | 10–20% | 10–20%+ | 10–20%+ | ~15–25% | Low | **5–8%** |
| Store-branded storefront | No | No | No | No | No | **Yes** |
| Seller owns customer data | No | No | No | No | No | **Yes (store CRM)** |
| COD | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

## What to copy
1. **Blinkit/Zepto:** real-time inventory truth (never sell what isn't in stock); one-tap reorder; crisp order-status timeline.
2. **BigBasket:** slot-based delivery selection (fits our fulfilment model better than 10-min).
3. **JioMart:** kirana-partner fulfilment playbook — the store is the warehouse.

## What to beat
1. **Seller economics:** zero onboarding fee + 5–8% commission vs 10–20% + heavy ad spend.
2. **Store identity:** each partner gets their own branded shop URL, not a listing buried in a marketplace.
3. **Data ownership:** store sees its own customers and repeat rates — incumbents hide this.
