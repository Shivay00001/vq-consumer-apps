# Competitor Research — VQ Jobs

Research date: 2026-09-26. Prices per cited sources; verify before publishing.

## Competitors

### 1. Naukri.com
- **Model:** India's largest job portal; 10 crore+ profiles, 7 lakh+ businesses (HROne 2026).
- **Employer pricing:** Free posting (1 active post, 50 applications, 7-day validity); Classified ₹850/post (₹400 non-metro); Hot Vacancy ₹1,650/post; SMB plans ₹2,500/mo (2 posts) / ₹3,500 (3 posts) / ₹4,950 (5 posts) + GST; Resdex Lite ~₹4,000+ for limited CV views (Internshala, HROne).
- **Copy:** unmatched candidate volume; Resdex database; NVite outreach; iimjobs/Hirist sister sites.
- **Beat:** free unlimited posting at launch; simpler UX; no database upsell pressure on small employers.

### 2. LinkedIn
- **Model:** professional network + hiring; precision sourcing via filters, InMail.
- **Employer pricing:** **1 free job slot** per active requisition; Recruiter Lite ~**₹8,260/month** (~30 InMails); full Recruiter custom-quoted (HROne 2026).
- **Copy:** passive-candidate sourcing quality; profile-as-resume.
- **Beat:** LinkedIn is overkill/overpriced for SMB volume hiring; VQ is the "post in 3 minutes, free" alternative.

### 3. Apna
- **Model:** blue/grey-collar + frontline hiring, app-first; 50M+ candidates, 900+ cities; AI calling agent for screening.
- **Employer pricing:** roughly **₹1,949/month** (few job credits) up to **₹7,099** six-month plan (HROne 2026).
- **Copy:** AI screening calls for high-volume hiring; hyperlocal matching.
- **Beat/learn:** Apna proves employers pay for *screening automation*, not just listings — copy the idea later (WhatsApp-based screening via VQ's own outreach stack). Don't fight Apna on blue-collar turf initially; own SMB generalist hiring.

### 4. Indeed (India)
- **Model:** aggregator + direct postings; free posting with pay-per-click sponsored boost.
- **Pricing:** free to post; sponsored jobs from ~₹30–50/day budgets (self-serve, varies).
- **Copy:** sponsored-job model (pay only for visibility) is the honest middle ground between free and recruiter seats.
- **Beat/learn:** copy the sponsored/boost mechanic for the Featured listing tier (₹199–499 flat is simpler for Indian SMBs than PPC).

### 5. Cutshort / Wellfound (niche)
- **Model:** Cutshort: tech hiring with AI matching, pay-per-hire; Wellfound: startup jobs, free, equity-transparent.
- **Pricing:** Cutshort charges on successful hire (percentage-ish/credits); Wellfound free for startups.
- **Copy:** quality-over-quantity positioning; transparent salary/equity norms.
- **Beat:** VQ's wedge is breadth + free, not niche depth.

## What to copy
1. **Naukri:** moderation + "report job" trust infrastructure; application status visibility for candidates.
2. **Indeed:** sponsored/boost as the monetisation mechanic (flat-fee variant for India).
3. **Apna:** screening automation as the Phase-2 upsell (AI call/WhatsApp screen before interview).

## What to beat
1. **Price:** free posting with no 7-day expiry games and no per-post toll — the SMB wedge.
2. **Speed:** post-a-job in 3 minutes, mobile-first (the prototype already does this).
3. **Trust:** pre-publish moderation + verified-employer badges from day one — fake-job spam is the #1 reason small boards die.
