# Competitor Research — VQ Food

Research date: 2026-09-26. Prices per cited sources; verify before quoting to restaurants.

## Competitors

### 1. Zomato
- **Model:** food delivery + dining; ~50%+ share of Indian online food delivery with Swiggy.
- **Restaurant pricing:** commissions **16–30%** of order value depending on scale (smaller restaurants pay more); advertising/promo fees on top, no cap (Financial Express, 2026). Platform fee to customer: **₹14.90/order** (News18, Mar 2026). Reported extras: long-distance fee ₹20 (4–6 km) / ₹40 (>6 km) per order; "uptime pack" ₹250–750/week for visibility (rolled back after pushback — Mint, Aug 2025).
- **Copy:** discovery/SEO dominance, review corpus, rider network density, Gold membership.
- **Beat:** commission cap (12% all-in), transparent fee math, restaurant owns repeat-customer channel.

### 2. Swiggy
- **Model:** food delivery + Instamart quick commerce + Dineout.
- **Restaurant pricing:** commissions **15–25%** typical (News18); onboarding fee/deposit reported ~₹999 or ₹1,000–2,000 security deposit deducted gradually (MentorPOS). Platform fee to customer: **₹14.99/order** (News18, Mar 2026). Delivery partners earn ₹20–50/order (+ incentives).
- **Copy:** logistics tech (assignment, batching), subscription (Swiggy One), partner app maturity.
- **Beat:** same as Zomato — take-rate. Plus onboarding cost friction for small restaurants.

### 3. Flipkart (upcoming food delivery)
- **Model:** entering food delivery 2026 with **commission cap of 15–20% including ad spend** (Financial Express, citing sources); pilot offered **10–11% commission** to ~300 Bengaluru restaurants with menu-price parity mandate; OTP-based consent for discounting (restaurant approves campaigns).
- **Copy:** price-parity mandate, consent-based discounting — both are restaurant-friendly precedents.
- **Beat:** VQ caps lower (12%) and launches without a giant's bureaucracy; copy the consent-based discounting idea.

### 4. ONDC (Open Network for Digital Commerce)
- **Model:** open protocol; restaurants list once, sell via any buyer app; take-rates reported far below aggregators.
- **Copy:** interoperability, low take-rate philosophy.
- **Beat/learn:** ONDC validates that restaurants want out of 25%+ commissions. Consider ONDC seller-app integration as a Phase-3 distribution channel rather than competing head-on.

### 5. MagicPin / Thrive (direct-ordering challengers)
- **Model:** MagicPin: discovery + delivery with lower commissions; Thrive: commission-free direct ordering (SaaS fee to restaurant instead).
- **Copy:** Thrive's "zero commission, SaaS fee" model proves restaurants will pay for software instead of per-order tax.
- **Beat:** offer both — capped commission for marketplace orders + flat SaaS (Restaurant Pro) for direct/repeat orders.

## Feature matrix

| Feature | Zomato | Swiggy | Flipkart (upcoming) | ONDC | **VQ Food (target)** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commission | 16–30% | 15–25% | 10–11% pilot, cap 15–20% | Low | **Cap 12% all-in** |
| Onboarding fee | — | ~₹999/deposit | — | — | **₹0** |
| Live rider tracking | Yes | Yes | TBD | Via apps | Yes (Realtime+GPS) |
| Restaurant owns customer data | Limited | Limited | TBD | Yes | **Yes** |
| Discount consent | Platform-driven | Platform-driven | OTP consent | — | **Restaurant approves** |
| Direct/repeat channel | No | No | No | — | **Yes (Pro)** |

## What to copy
1. **Swiggy:** dispatch tech — assignment, batching, ETA math; rider app with upfront payout display.
2. **Zomato:** review corpus + photos as trust layer; membership mechanics later.
3. **Flipkart:** OTP/consent-based discounting; menu-price parity as trust signal.

## What to beat
1. **Take-rate:** 12% capped, published — the single biggest restaurant pain point (Mint: "nearly half the order value disappears").
2. **Fee transparency:** show the restaurant the exact split per order (commission, gateway, delivery) — incumbents are criticised for "hidden costs".
3. **Repeat-customer ownership:** restaurant keeps its regulars on a low-fee direct channel instead of paying discovery tax forever.
